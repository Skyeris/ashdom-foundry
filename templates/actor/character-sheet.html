CRITICAL CHANCE

Adds to the normal Success Cap of 5.

Negative values lower the beginning of the Failure Range.

VARIANT RULES

Applies Critical Chance to Skill, ST, and WP checks.

Caps Acrobatics, Athletics, Chance, Logic, and Perception according to their FORMULA Total.

Multiplies every FORMULA Skill Limit by 5, increasing the 10+ limit from 40 to 200.

Replaces the Base 5 with each skill's FORMULA equation, divided by two and rounded up. The five primary-derived skills are excluded.

Sub-rule of FORMULA Skill Equations. LCK/2 is rounded up.

Adds a separate Horror tracker for exposure to psychic disturbances and reality-bending phenomena that steadily deteriorate a character's mental state.

Converts d20 rolls to d100, changes standard Skill Bases to 25, multiplies the five primary-derived Skill Bases, Fatigue penalties, and Total ST/WP by 5, changes SP to 50, recalculates Critical Chance, and raises the maximum Total AC to 150. Other values remain as entered. It cannot be combined with FORMULA Skill Equations.

Replaces the single Crippled status with separate Head, Torso, Arm, Leg, and Groin crippled locations.
